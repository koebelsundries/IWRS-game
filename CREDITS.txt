IWRS (Injured Wildlife Rescue Squad) — credits
================================================

Player and animal sprites
  Drawn by Donny for this game (girl and boy walk cycles, tabby cat,
  tuxedo cat). Sliced from the supplied strips, downscaled with an
  alpha-aware box filter, and packed into assets/sprites.png
  (map: assets/sprites.json and assets/sprites.js).

Front and back walk cycles (walk down / walk up)
  Generated to match Donny's boy and girl side-view art (same outfits,
  colors, hair, backpack and boots), then background-removed, sliced,
  and downscaled into assets/sprites.png with the existing side frames.


Forest tiles, trees, bushes, rocks, flowers, the ravine and bridge
  Original procedural pixel art drawn in the game (no third-party kits).

Icon
  The sitting tabby from Donny's sprite strip, placed on a parchment /
  sage background for the PWA icons in icons/.

Font
  Press Start 2P by CodeMan38 (cody@zone38.net)
  SIL Open Font License 1.1 — see fonts/OFL.txt
  Unmodified copy from https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/pressstart2p

Sound effects and music
  Synthesised in the browser with WebAudio (no audio files).

No assets from any commercial game were used.
